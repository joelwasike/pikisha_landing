Privacy Policy

Data Collection

We collect only essential information needed to deliver your orders: name, email, delivery address, and payment details. Your data is encrypted and stored securely.

Your information is used solely to process orders, improve our service, and communicate with you about your meals. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

Your Rights

You can request to view, update, or delete your personal data at any time. Contact us at privacy@pikisha.com for any data-related inquiries.
